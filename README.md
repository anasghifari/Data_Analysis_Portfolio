## Project Spotlight — Brazilian E-Commerce Analytics

> *Analyzing 100,000+ orders from Olist, Brazil's largest marketplace (2016–2018)*

**Business Questions Answered:**
- Which product categories drive the most revenue (GMV)?
- What is the monthly revenue growth trend?
- Which states have the worst delivery performance?
- What is the customer retention rate?
- How do payment methods affect order value?

**Key Findings:**
- Health & Beauty leads with **R$1.26M GMV** — 15% of total platform revenue
- Only **~3% of customers** place repeat orders — a major retention opportunity
- All 27 Brazilian states receive deliveries **ahead of schedule** on average
- Credit card users spend **2.5x more** than boleto users due to installments

**[View Live Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/shared/8YGYRXJ9N)**
